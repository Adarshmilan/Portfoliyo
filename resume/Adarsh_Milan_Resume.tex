\section{Research (Ongoing)}
\entry{Relapse-Guard: Stopping Knowledge Relapse in Continual Learning--Unlearning}{Oct 2026 -- Present}
\textit{PyTorch, ResNet-18, CIFAR-10/100, Continual Learning, Machine Unlearning}\par
\begin{points}
  \item Studying Continual Learning--Unlearning (CLU), where a model receives a stream of ``learn'' and ``forget'' requests. Builds on UnCLe (Adhikari et al., 2025), which showed that forgotten knowledge often \emph{relapses} when the model later learns similar tasks.
  \item Proposed method: store a PCA ``forget signature'' of each deleted class's feature directions and add a \textbf{guard loss} during later training, so the network cannot rebuild those directions. No forgotten images are kept.
  \item Plan to verify forgetting \emph{inside the features} with linear probes and membership-inference attacks, not only at the output.
  \item Completed so far: a ResNet-18 training pipeline, a Split CIFAR-100 class-incremental benchmark (10 tasks), a reproduction of catastrophic forgetting, and Elastic Weight Consolidation and experience-replay baselines, with all runs logged as accuracy matrices.
  \item Next steps: DER++, unlearning baselines (BadTeacher), spill and relapse metrics, then the full Relapse-Guard method and ablations, with the aim of a paper.
\end{points}
